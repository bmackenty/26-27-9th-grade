What else can AdminLTE demos show?

Charts, calendars, maps, rich text editors and enhanced searchable tables need additional libraries and often data or server code. They are not installed in our Lesson 8 vendor folder. A dashboard template does not automatically implement these features.

Full page layouts such as login, invoice, profile and mailbox are compositions of components. They do not supply authentication, payments or email services. Icons need an icon library; this gallery uses words and simple symbols.

Header, sidebar, content and footer form the application shell: inspect your existing dashboard.html for a working full-layout example.
